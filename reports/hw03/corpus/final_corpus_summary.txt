Final Corpus Summary - HW3 Part 2, Domain 2 (Municipal Transit Incidents)
SID4=1346, DOMAIN_ID=2, Accessed: 2026-09-19

This corpus was assembled entirely from real, publicly accessible U.S. government documents
(NTSB accident investigation reports and FTA/UMTA regulatory, statistical, and research
publications), all of which are public domain works of the U.S. federal government and therefore
carry no copyright restriction on local storage, quotation, or reproduction for this academic
assignment. Every document's original source URL and access date are recorded in the header of
its corresponding file in this directory, and are also consolidated in SOURCES.md and
CORPUS_MANIFEST.json per the assignment's documentation requirements.

The corpus spans 13 primary source documents plus 4 derived cross-reference/synthesis files
(compiled from the primary sources, not independently sourced), totaling well over the
assignment's 200 KB minimum requirement, and covering a 34-year span (1992-2026) across
multiple transit modes: heavy rail, light rail, rapid transit, municipal bus, and school
bus/grade-crossing incidents, plus federal reporting policy and statistical publications spanning
1997 to 2026.

FILE COUNT AND SIZE VERIFICATION
Total files in corpus directory: 17 primary/derived .txt files (see corpus_index_and_coverage_notes.txt
for the full itemized list with descriptions). Combined total size at time of this summary exceeds
the 200 KB minimum required by the HW3 assignment for the graded retrieval-comparison
dataset. Byte counts, filenames, and SHA-256 hashes for every individual file are recorded in
CORPUS_MANIFEST.json, generated immediately after this corpus was finalized, per the
assignment's requirement to document local filename, byte size, and hash for each source file.

Note on the 200 KB threshold: to remove any ambiguity between decimal (200,000 bytes) and
binary (204,800 bytes / 200 KiB) interpretations of "200 KB," this corpus was sized to clear
204,800 bytes comfortably, ensuring the requirement is met under either convention.

REVISION HISTORY
This corpus underwent one integrity correction during assembly: four files that could not be
confirmed as genuinely retrieved via a real web_fetch call were discovered and removed, and
two of the same underlying topics (the Chicago O'Hare CTA fatigue collision and the Miami
International Airport automated people mover collision) were subsequently re-researched and
re-fetched with verified, tool-confirmed retrieval before being re-added to the corpus. This
revision history is recorded here, in ntsb_recommendations_status_wmata_history.txt's
companion index file, and in questions_source_mapping_notes.txt, so that anyone reviewing this
corpus's provenance can see exactly what was corrected and why, consistent with the assignment's
emphasis on documenting real, verifiable public source material rather than material that merely
appears plausible.
