SECURITY -- WHERE TRANSIT CRIMES OCCUR (Part I offenses, rail vs. bus contrast)
For BUS, 63.9% of all Part I crime offenses occurred inside vehicles, versus only 17.8% in
stations. For RAIL, the pattern inverts: only 12.7% of Part I offenses occurred inside vehicles,
while 34.0% occurred in stations and 53.2% occurred in "Other" locations (parking facilities,
platforms, right-of-way, etc.) -- suggesting rail crime is far more concentrated in station
environments and surrounding areas than inside the moving vehicle itself, the opposite of the bus
pattern.
