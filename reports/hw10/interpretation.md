**Evidence:** 54 traces reached the evaluator; 51 were scored and 3 were error records (2 planner dead-letters while Ollama was down, 1 injected Writer fault). Mean GEval scores (1-5): Plan 1.86, Writer draft 2.18, Final answer 2.20.

- **The Reviewer adds latency but changes nothing.** It approved 51/51 drafts and returned the draft text unchanged every time (`identical_text = true` for all 51). It costs 1.5 s at p50 per request, and the final answer cannot be better than the draft. The judge still gave the identical text a different score in 20 of 51 traces (from -3 to +3): that is judge noise, not improvement.
- **The Planner is the weakest stage (mean 1.86, 40/51 scored 2).** Plans mostly restate the database record ("note that no passengers were affected") instead of planning the answer. For "What is the route title for INC-000512?" the plan never mentions the route title. The Writer scores slightly higher (2.18) because it reads the records in the question directly, so it partly makes up for the plan.
- **Coordination takeaway:** the three agents do not check each other. The Reviewer should compare the draft with the question and the records, and either correct the draft or reject it; the Planner should receive the question type, so a fact lookup gets a one-step "state field X" plan.
- **Caveat:** the judge disagrees with the human labels (Spearman -0.26) and changes its score on 55% of cases between runs, so these numbers point a direction but are not reliable enough to gate a deployment.
